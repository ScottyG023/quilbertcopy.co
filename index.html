What they say

Services

What I'm usually brought in for.

Copywriting. Websites, brand stories, video scripts, blogs, social posts, sales brochures, flyers, product labels and case studies. Give me the audience, the product and what makes it different, and the process is research, write, review, polish.

Editing and proofing. Books, e-books, news reports, speech and video scripts, business tenders, awards submissions and biographies. Whether that's a light proof or a structural rewrite, you'll get told which one it actually needs.

Event promotion. Press releases, invitations, posters and flyers for launches, fixtures, fundraisers and every other kind of do.

Working with agencies, I'm happy on white label, to your tone guidelines, and in your client's voice rather than mine. The rules stay the same regardless: be clear, be concise, and never send the reader to the land of nod.

How it runs

What you get, every time.

  • A plan, a price and a delivery date, agreed before anything starts.
  • Proper research of the subject, including interviews where they help.
  • Sample copy first, whenever you'd rather see the tone before committing.
  • A first draft, then every amend you've marked up, done.
  • Spelling, grammar, punctuation and tense checked, then a final proof.
  • One consistent tone from start to finish, ready to release.

About

I. Heart. Words.

But effective writing takes more than loving the language. There's craft in it, obviously: a decent vocabulary, a grounding in grammar, some creativity. What matters equally is a flair for connecting with people. Listening, empathising and re-telling someone's story in a way that's accurate and actually worth reading.

Add the 20+ years I've spent in communication, marketing, research and sales, and you've got a winning combination.

Finding typos and grammatical faux pas is the part I enjoy most, which probably tells you everything.
